The Craft · Since 2014

A small batch is a honest batch.

Every morning at four, Marco walks the back stair with a single bucket of milk from a farm thirty minutes east. The cream is folded by hand into a copper drum that holds exactly twelve liters — small enough that no flavor ever has to wait.

We never use stabilizers, never use paste, never use dye. If a flavor runs out by 11pm, it runs out. The board changes. The night changes. That is the whole point.

Visit · Hawthorne Lane

Find the door with no sign.

An unmarked black door on Hawthorne Lane, between the wine shop and the tailor. Knock once. If the light above the door is on, we are open.

Reservations are not taken. We hold a counter of fourteen seats, six stools, and one corner booth. First come, first poured.
